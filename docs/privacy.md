# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 28, 2026
**Application:** REIN-BOOKS-SETUP (internal QuickBooks Online integration for Rein Farms LLC)

## 1. Overview

REIN-BOOKS-SETUP ("the App") is an internal-use tool developed by the owner of Rein Farms LLC to access and analyze QuickBooks Online ("QBO") data belonging to Rein Farms LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is stored, and how it is protected.

## 2. Information the App Accesses

When connected to QuickBooks Online, the App may access:
- Company information (legal name, address, fiscal year settings, tax identifiers)
- Customer and vendor records
- Chart of accounts
- Invoices, bills, payments, deposits, journal entries
- Items, classes, and locations
- Financial reports (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, General Ledger, Trial Balance)
- Payroll data, where the connected company has Payroll enabled

The App accesses only data belonging to QuickBooks Online companies the connecting user is authorized to administer.

## 3. How Information is Used

Accessed data is used solely for:
- Internal financial review and bookkeeping
- Preparation of federal and state tax returns
- Generation of reports for the company owner

Data is **not** sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or processed for any purpose other than those listed above.

## 4. Storage and Security

- OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the App owner's machine in restricted-access files. Tokens are never committed to source control.
- Any extracted financial data persisted by the App (for example, in CSV or local SQLite files) is stored only on devices controlled by the App owner.
- All API communication with Intuit occurs over HTTPS.
- The App does not transmit accessed data to any server other than Intuit's QuickBooks Online API endpoints.

## 5. Data Retention

Locally stored data is retained for as long as needed for tax filing, audit support, and ongoing bookkeeping. The App owner may delete local copies at any time. Disconnecting the App from QuickBooks Online revokes its API access.

## 6. Third Parties

The App relies on Intuit's QuickBooks Online API. Use of the App is also subject to Intuit's privacy practices, available at https://www.intuit.com/privacy/. The App does not integrate with any other third-party services that receive QBO data.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adult business use and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in App functionality or legal requirements. The "Effective Date" above will be revised on any update.
